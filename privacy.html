Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Who we are

Zyclipra is a free Windows app for recording clips, mixing audio and playing a soundboard, with an optional companion website at madaai.github.io/zyclipra-dashboard. It is made by an independent developer, “MaDaAi” (“we”), in Ontario, Canada. Contact: notefirasupport@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We don't run any servers that receive your data. We don't sell, share or analyse your data, and there's no advertising or analytics.
  • Your clips, settings and recordings stay on your PC, and in your own Google Drive only if you turn that on.
  • The website talks to Google (to sign you in and show your Drive clips) and directly to your own PC on your home network.

What the Zyclipra app processes (on your PC)

  • Recordings, screenshots and clips of your screen, game audio and microphone – only when you start recording or instant replay. Stored in the folder you choose.
  • Settings, including sound names and shortcuts, stored in your Windows user folder.
  • Dashboard sign-in: a username and a password hashed with Argon2id (the password itself is never stored).
  • Google account details if you connect Google Drive: your name, email address and profile picture (to show who is signed in), and a Google refresh token kept in Windows Credential Manager.

The app has no telemetry and sends nothing to us. It only contacts Google's servers (when you use Google Drive) and devices you allow on your network.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, Zyclipra asks only for the drive.file permission, so it can see and change only the files Zyclipra itself made in your Drive – nothing else. It uploads the clips you choose (or new clips, if you turn on automatic upload) to a “Zyclipra” folder, and keeps two small private files there that let the website find and connect to your PC (your PC's home-network address, a random link key and its certificate fingerprint). You can delete these files or disconnect at any time.

Zyclipra's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide Zyclipra's features to you, is never transferred to us or anyone else, is never used for advertising, and is never read by a person.

What the website processes

  • Google sign-in through Google Identity Services. The access token stays in the page's memory and is gone when you close the tab.
  • Your Zyclipra files on Google Drive – listed and played in your browser. Thumbnails are loaded from Google's servers.
  • Your PC – on the same network the website connects straight to it (WebRTC, encrypted). The connection setup is passed through the private file in your Google Drive; nothing passes through us.
  • Hosting: the website is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.

See the Cookie Policy for what the website stores in your browser.

Canadian privacy law

We follow the principles of Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA): we collect only what a feature needs, use it only for that feature, keep it under your control, and protect it. Because Zyclipra's data stays on your devices and in your own Google account, we do not hold personal information about you. If you believe we've mishandled your information you can contact us, and you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Legal bases (where GDPR / UK GDPR apply)

Processing happens on your own devices and in your own Google account to provide the features you ask for (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b)), and to keep the service secure (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f)). We do not receive your personal data, so we are not able to access, sell or disclose it.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete clips and settings on your PC at any time; uninstalling removes the app.
  • Disconnect Google Drive in the app, and remove Zyclipra's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Turn off “Zyclipra website” in the app, or press “Reset website link”, to stop the website using your PC.
  • Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object or restrict processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because your data stays on your devices and in your Google account, you can exercise most of these yourself; contact us for anything else.

Children

Zyclipra is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 (or the minimum age to consent to data processing in your country) to use Google sign-in with Zyclipra.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS, WebRTC/DTLS), passwords are hashed with Argon2id, tokens are kept in Windows Credential Manager or only in memory, and remote access is off until you turn it on. No system is perfectly secure; keep your PC, browser and Zyclipra up to date.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Email notefirasupport@gmail.com for questions, privacy requests or complaints. We aim to reply within 30 days. Zyclipra is made in Ontario, Canada.
