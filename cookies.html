The Zyclipra website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. That's why there is no cookie banner.

Google

Signing in loads Google Identity Services from accounts.google.com, and clip thumbnails come from Google's servers. Google may use its own cookies on its own sites when you sign in; see how Google uses cookies.

Clearing it

Clear this site's data in your browser settings at any time. The website keeps working; the streaming helper is set up again next time.
